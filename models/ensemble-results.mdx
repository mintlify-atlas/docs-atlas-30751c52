# Ensemble results

The production ensemble combines 5 independently trained `SuperEnhancedTemporalModel` checkpoints (ensemble\_model\_0 through ensemble\_model\_4). Predictions are aggregated by averaging softmax probabilities across all members before taking the argmax.

<Info>
  ensemble\_model\_0 failed to converge (best validation accuracy 25.8%) and is excluded from the checkpoint selection table below. The final evaluation ensemble uses all 5 checkpoints as recorded in `ensemble_final_results.json`.
</Info>

## Test-Time Augmentation

TTA applies multiple perturbations to each input at inference time and averages the resulting probability distributions. Its impact varies by member.

| Model | Standard accuracy | TTA accuracy | TTA delta |
|-------|------------------|-------------|----------|
| ensemble\_model\_1 | 74.51% | 74.51% | 0.00 pp |
| ensemble\_model\_2 | 92.48% | 91.75% | −0.73 pp |
| ensemble\_model\_3 | 91.75% | 91.99% | +0.24 pp |
| ensemble\_model\_4 | 93.45% | 93.20% | −0.25 pp |

<Note>
  TTA consistently benefits ensemble\_model\_3 but slightly degrades ensemble\_model\_2 and ensemble\_model\_4. TTA benefit depends on model calibration and the augmentation strategy used. Apply TTA selectively or evaluate on a validation hold-out before enabling it in production.
</Note>

## Why ensemble_model_1 is included

ensemble\_model\_1 has a low overall accuracy (72.73% validation, 74.51% test) and fails entirely on Animation at test time. Its inclusion in the ensemble is justified by:

1. **Flat\_Content strength** — 93.47% validation F1, among the highest of any model on that class.
2. **Natural\_Content strength** — 96.69% validation F1, comparable to models 2–4.
3. **Gaming coverage** — 66.06% validation F1, providing complementary signal when models 2–4 disagree.
4. **Prediction diversity** — A lower-accuracy model trained to a different local optimum can reduce ensemble variance on classes where the other members are also uncertain, as long as it does not introduce systematic errors that the averaging cannot cancel.

<Tip>
  If inference latency is a concern, removing ensemble\_model\_1 from the ensemble is the lowest-cost pruning option. Models 2–4 alone reach ~92–93% accuracy with full Animation coverage.
</Tip>
